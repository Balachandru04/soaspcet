Anti-Ragging Committee

Complaint & Action Taken Report

The Anti-ragging committee constituted by the Chairman of the committee met on 24/11/22 at Conference Hall, to review and strengthen the measures to curb the menace of ragging in the Institute for the session 2022-23.

The Committee appraised the UGC regulations on curbing the menace of ragging in higher educational institutions 2009 and other instructions issued as per the directions of the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India and the State Government regulations. Students were informed about anti-ragging measures through the prospectus, hostel rules, and posters displayed across the campus.

Recommendations:

  • Periodic review by the Anti Ragging Committee with action recommendations to the Anti Ragging Squad.
  • Close monitoring of campus entry to prevent anti-social elements from entering.
  • Squad vigilance during break times and at entry/exit points.
  • Immediate reporting of any incident to the Principal by staff or committee members.
  • Faculty to remind students regularly about consequences of ragging.
  • Classes should never be left unattended.
  • Bus in-charges to monitor for ragging incidents inside buses.
  • Squad members to conduct frequent campus rounds.
  • Visitor monitoring inside the campus.
  • Hostel wardens to patrol at night and prevent senior-fresher room visits.
  • Annual meeting at academic year start, with additional meetings as required.

Next meeting date: 21.07.2023 at 10:30 AM (or earlier if needed).

Complaints cum Redressal Committee

About the Committee

The Complaints cum Redressal Committee is established to address grievances of students, faculty, and staff promptly and fairly. The committee ensures that all complaints are examined in a confidential and impartial manner, providing suitable recommendations for resolution. It plays a vital role in fostering a positive campus environment by upholding the values of justice, transparency, and respect for all.
