HOSTEL

About Hostel

Our hostel is located in the city’s finest and trendiest district. Everyone who is interested in being a part of the creative and cutting-edge world that is Chennai, you are welcome to St. Peter’s hostel for the best experience.

  • Separate hostels are available for boys and girls with 164 and 156 rooms respectively with the total building area of 17,000 sq.m.
  • Rooms are available for single, double, triple and quadruple accommodation with attached bathrooms.
  • Kitchens are well equipped with modern steam cooking systems.

Hostel Life

Hostel life is full of opportunities to meet new friends, participate in events, and create memories that will last a lifetime. We encourage community building and personal growth.

  • Clean and hygienic dining halls are maintained throughout.
  • Hostels are provided with reading rooms, TV rooms, and recreation halls.
  • A doctor visits the college/hostels daily to provide medical attention for students in need.

HEALTHCARE SUPPORT SERVICES
St.Peter’s College

A primary health center is operating for preliminary treatment to sick students in our campus. The Health center possesses enough drugs, beds and other medication facilities for needy persons. An ambulance is always ready at student’s service.

  • Medical Facilities available in the Campus.
  • Free Medicines are provided to sick students.
  • College ambulance is in availability for 24×7.

STUDENT SPORTS FACILITIES

At St.Peter’s College of Engineering and Technology, we prioritize sports alongside academics to foster holistic development. Our expansive 30-acre Sports Village provides a dedicated space for athletic activities. Equipped with top-notch facilities, it serves as the training ground for our talented students.

We encourage participation in inter-university and intra-university tournaments, supporting our students with resources and guidance. Our college teams have consistently triumphed in major sporting events, including the renowned Anna University Inter Zonal Sports Championship among 575 engineering colleges.

Through sports, we promote values like sportsmanship, resilience, and teamwork, enhancing personal growth. At St.Peter’s College of Engineering and Technology, we strike a balance between academics and sports, recognizing their significance for success and well-being.

Join St.Peter’s College of Engineering and Technology Sports to unlock your potential, forge lasting friendships, and make your mark in the sports world!
