Foreword

School of Architecture, St.Peter’s College of Engineering and Technology, Chennai, is a reputed institution approved by COA, New Delhi and affiliated to Anna University, Chennai. The Institution offers B.Arch program with an annual intake of 80 students.

Director's Message

Learning is a continuous process and does not end with the acquisition of a degree, especially because steady and rapid advances in architecture shorten the life of tools and techniques prevalent today. Therefore we do not aim to make our students walking manuals of any specific standards. Instead, they are given a strong foundation in architecture and design-solving techniques, and are made adaptable to changes. Our understanding is that persons who are clear and thorough about the fundamentals of architectural detailing can adapt to rapid changes in technology relatively easily. We want the education imparted to our students to be the basis of a lifetime of learning. I attribute this success to the winning combination of a dedicated faculty that works hard at imparting quality education, a well-planned syllabus, and last but not least, our students. We believe that this approach to teaching-learning, coupled with practical experience gained during Practical Training in reputed organizations, equips our students to handle the challenges posed by the industry.

About Us

The School of Architecture at St. Peter's College of Engineering and Technology in Chennai is a reputed institution approved by the Council of Architecture, New Delhi, and affiliated with Anna University. We offer a B.Arch program with an annual intake of 80 students, supported by state-of-the-art facilities: design studios, air-conditioned computer labs, material museum, well-equipped workshops for model making, clay modeling, carpentry, climatology, and a library with extensive reference materials and the latest national and international journals.

Our Rankholders

Vision

To be a leading center of excellence in architectural education that nurtures innovation, creativity, and sustainability, producing globally competent architects who can address the complex challenges of the built environment. We envision a future where our graduates are not only skilled designers but also responsible citizens committed to improving the quality of life through architecture that is socially responsive, environmentally sustainable, and culturally sensitive.

Mission

  • To provide a dynamic and inspiring learning environment that integrates theoretical knowledge with hands-on practice in design studios, workshops, and fieldwork.
  • To foster creativity, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills through exposure to real-world projects, interdisciplinary learning, and collaborative work culture.
  • To promote research, innovation, and sustainable design approaches that address the needs of modern society and future generations.
  • To uphold ethical values and professional integrity, encouraging students to become leaders in the field of architecture.
  • To build strong industry and community partnerships that enhance learning, provide internship opportunities, and ensure graduates are career-ready.

Objectives

  • To produce architects with strong design, technical, and problem-solving skills.
  • To foster innovation and sustainable design solutions.
  • To prepare students for leadership roles in the profession.

Salient Features

  • Central Chennai location, easily accessible by trains and buses.
  • Transport facilities for students and staff.
  • Modern laboratories with the latest design and drafting software.
  • Regular field trips, site visits, and live project exposure.
  • Library with architectural books, periodicals, and e-resources.
  • Specialized labs for climatology, structures, and surveying.

Workshops

Outdoor construction yards and specialized workshops help students gain hands-on experience in building techniques, model making, and other crafts essential for architectural practice.

Studios

Our design studios are vibrant spaces for creativity, equipped with drafting tables, model-making tools, and digital workstations for architectural visualization.

Why Architecture?

Architecture offers a unique combination of creativity, technical skill, and problem-solving. It is a profession that shapes our physical world, impacting communities and the environment.

Scope of Architecture

Graduates can work in design firms, construction companies, urban planning organizations, and government agencies, or pursue higher studies in specialized fields like urban design, landscape architecture, and conservation.

Career Opportunities

  • Urban Planner
  • Interior Designer
  • Landscape Architect
  • Project Manager
  • Academician / Researcher
