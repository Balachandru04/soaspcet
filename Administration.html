ADMINISTRATION

School of Architecture, St. Peter’s College of Engineering and Technology, Chennai

Overview

St.Peter’s College of Engineering and Technology, a co-educational institution was established by Lakshmi Saraswathi Educational Trust in the year 2008. The college aims to impart training to students to develop their Intellectual powers, identify and cultivate interest and talents, and train them to become responsible and eminent citizens of India. This institute provides various engineering disciplines, focusing on learning, industry engagement of students, innovative and inclusive pedagogy, and ethics. While students at St.Peter’s engross themselves in academics, the college has a lot in store for them outside the classroom. Student life includes participation in sports, recreational & co-curricular activities and culturals.

Chairperson’s Message

Architecture is more than designing spaces — it is about shaping communities and enriching human experiences. At our School of Architecture, we aim to provide holistic education that not only develops technical skills but also fosters cultural sensitivity, environmental responsibility, and innovative thinking. I encourage every student to dream big, think creatively, and work passionately towards making a positive impact.

Secretary’s Message

The role of architecture in the modern world is constantly evolving. Our institution ensures that students are exposed to both traditional design principles and cutting-edge technological advancements. We emphasize hands-on learning, industry collaboration, and interdisciplinary projects to prepare students for the dynamic challenges of the profession.

Director’s Message

Learning is a continuous process and does not end with the acquisition of a degree, especially because steady and rapid advances in architecture shorten the life of tools and techniques prevalent today. Therefore we do not aim to make our students walking manuals of any specific standards. Instead, they are given a strong foundation in architecture and design-solving techniques, and are made adaptable to changes. Our understanding is that persons who are clear and thorough about the fundamentals of architectural detailing can adapt to rapid changes in technology relatively easily. We want the education imparted to our students to be the basis of a life time of learning. I attribute this success to the winning combination of a dedicated faculty that works hard at imparting quality education, a well-planned syllabus and last but not least, our students. We believe that this approach to teaching-learning, coupled with practical experience gained during Practical Training in reputed organizations, equips our students to handle the challenges posed by the industry.
